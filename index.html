About us

Who we are

Panchakoshi Disability Development Forum, Dailekh was registered with the District Administration Office, Dailekh on 18 Phalgun 2062 BS. We help disadvantaged people gain equal access to, control over and ownership of natural, economic and social resources.

Our target groups are persons with disabilities and people who are socially and economically excluded. PDDF is a well-governed, transparent organization and a leading voice for disability rights advocacy in Dailekh.

  • Empowerment
  • Drinking water and sanitation
  • Micro-enterprise
  • Income generation
  • Small irrigation
  • Disaster risk reduction
  • Nutrition
  • Climate change

Our direction

Vision, mission and goal

Vision

A just and inclusive society where all persons with disabilities enjoy equal rights, opportunities and dignity, and lead self-reliant, fulfilling lives.

Mission

To improve the social, economic and cultural well-being of persons with disabilities through accessible programmes, community engagement and strategic advocacy, so that no one is left behind.

Goal

To expand access to inclusive social, economic, educational and livelihood opportunities, so persons with disabilities can be self-reliant, exercise their rights and take part fully in society.

What we aim for

Our objectives

  1. Make services accessible

    Design and deliver inclusive, fair and barrier-free services that improve the quality of life of persons with disabilities.

  2. Promote education and livelihoods

    Increase access to quality education, vocational training and lasting employment suited to each person's needs and goals.

  3. Build self-reliance

    Strengthen personal, social and financial capacity through skill development, peer support and independent living programmes.

  4. Raise community awareness

    Run regular awareness and sensitization campaigns that reduce stigma and build respect for disability rights.

  5. Advocate for systemic change

    Champion the rights, dignity and real participation of persons with disabilities in policy, governance and the public and private sectors.

  6. Build strong partnerships

    Work with government, civil society, development partners and the private sector for coordinated, sustainable disability-inclusive development.

  • Inclusivity · embracing diversity
  • Respect · dignity of every person
  • Empowerment · self-determination
  • Accountability · transparency and integrity
  • Collaboration · alliances for impact

Recent activities

News from our ongoing work, newest first.

    Expertise

    Climate-resilient, inclusive water and sanitation

    Around 80% of Nepal's people are at risk from climate-related disasters, and women, children and persons with disabilities are hit hardest. Through Beyond the Finish Line, PDDF led social mobilization with SNV Nepal and IWMI Nepal in four rural municipalities.

    • Mapped disaster-prone locations and prepared risk-reduction action plans.
    • Used the GESI Self-Assessment Tool in municipal plans and budgets.
    • Rebuilt gravity-fed water schemes with climate-adaptive technology.
    • Prepared water safety plans for each source.
    • Brought women entrepreneurs into water system maintenance.
    • Introduced accessible water-source and tap designs.

    Working with government

    We coordinate with the District Administration Office, District Police Office, District Coordination Committee, Social Development Office and other agencies to put disability rights laws into practice. For community-based rehabilitation we have worked with the Ministry of Women, Children and Senior Citizens and with ministries of Karnali Province.

    We help municipalities with disability data collection, individual plans, access to government services, strategic planning and disaster information guidelines.
